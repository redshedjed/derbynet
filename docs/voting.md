# Voting

Besides the speed awards, many events give design awards: Most Patriotic,
Coolest Car, Best in Show. DerbyNet can let the audience, or the racers, vote
for these on their phones.

Car photos make a big difference: without them, voters choose by car number
alone. See [Photos](photos.md).

## Setting up the ballot

1. Create the awards in the **Awards Editor** (Judging > Edit Awards), or
   import a list of them (Set-Up > Import Awards).
2. On **Judging**, click **Manage Ballot**.
3. For each award to vote on, choose how many votes each ballot gets for it,
   or leave it "Not on ballot". Allowing several votes per award helps with
   young racers who vote for their own car: they'll spend one vote on it, and
   the rest on others.
4. Optionally, set a **ballot password**, to limit voting to people you give it
   to (announce it, or put it on a display).
5. Set **Balloting is** to open when you want voting to start. Close it when
   you're done.

While voting is open, the awards on the ballot can't be edited.

## Voting

Once at least one award is on the ballot, the home page offers a **Vote!**
tile to everyone, no login needed. Voters pick an award, see the cars, tap a
photo to see it full size, and tick their choices. A voter can change their
votes while balloting is open, from the same browser.

To get people onto the network, the **Join Wi-Fi** kiosk page shows the
visitor Wi-Fi name and password (Settings > Visitor Wi-Fi), with a QR code.

## Choosing the winners

On **Judging**, an award with votes shows a **Votes** marker. Click it to see
the tally, and **Choose** the winner. You have the final say, whatever the
votes: you'll need it for ties, at least. Awards can also be assigned by
dragging, whether or not they were voted on.
